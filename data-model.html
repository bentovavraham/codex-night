How projects, contracts, and charges are structured in the system

How everything fits together

Everything flows down from a Project. Each project has one or more Contracts with vendors. Each contract can have change orders, T&M charges, expenses, and invoices.

🏗 Project — e.g. "Oaktrail and Main Estates"
📊 Budget Lines — internal budget by QuickBooks category (set once per project)
📋 Contract — one per vendor, e.g. "Bright View Engineering — $6,300 fixed"
🔄 Change Orders — formal scope/cost changes; each adds to the commitment
⏱ T&M Charges — individual time entries (hours × rate); tracks open-ended exposure
🧾 Expenses — reimbursables: travel, tolls, hotels, copies, etc.
💵 Invoices — actual billing documents from the vendor (3 types — see below)
Fixed Scope T&M Expense — only Fixed invoices count against the contract value

Invoice types — what counts against what

Fixed Scope
Lump-sum billing
The vendor is invoicing for work that was defined and priced in the original contract (or a change order). This is work you agreed to pay a set amount for.
✓ Counts against the contract's committed value.
Triggers an overspend warning if it would exceed the remaining balance.
T&M
Time & Materials billing
The vendor is invoicing for hours worked at an agreed rate — typically for open-ended tasks like engineering meetings, coordination, or inspection time.
✓ Does NOT erode the fixed contract value.
Shows as additional project cost beyond the committed scope.
Expense
Reimbursable expenses
The vendor is billing back costs they incurred on your behalf — mileage, tolls, hotel, printing, etc. These are pass-through costs, not contracted work.
✓ Does NOT erode the fixed contract value.
Shows as additional project cost alongside T&M.

How the financial numbers are calculated

Commitment
 Contract detail KPI
Original contract value + all approved change orders
This is the legal/contractual obligation — what you've signed for.
Total Exposure
 Contract detail KPI, project health
Commitment + approved T&M charges + approved expenses
Your best estimate of total spend including open-ended work.
Invoiced to Date
 Contract detail KPI
Sum of all Fixed-scope invoices in Approved / Pushed / Paid status
Shows % of commitment consumed. T&M and Expense invoices are shown separately alongside this number.
Remaining (contract)
 Contract remaining balance
Contract total_value − Fixed-scope invoices billed
T&M invoices do not reduce this number — they are additional.
T&M Billed
 Contract detail KPI (sub-label)
Sum of T&M + Expense invoices in Approved / Pushed / Paid status
Additional project cost beyond the fixed commitment. Does not trigger an overspend warning.
Paid
 Contract detail KPI
Sum of all invoices (any type) in Paid status
Cash actually out the door.
Internal Budget
 Contract detail KPI
earmarked_amount — set manually per contract
Your internal estimate including T&M exposure. Should always be ≥ contract value. If Total Exposure exceeds this, a "cost creep" flag appears.

User roles and permissions

RoleWhoWhat they can do
PM Project Managers Create projects, contracts, invoices, T&M charges, expenses, change orders. Give first approval to anything.
Partner Partners / senior staff Everything a PM can do, plus give second (partner) approval.
Admin Seth Everything. Final (third) approval authority on all documents. Only admin can give final approval on invoices and change orders.

Audit trail

Every action on an invoice or contract is logged with who did it, when, and what changed. This includes approvals, rejections, edits, holds, and reverts. The history tab on any contract or invoice shows the full timeline.

Claude's document extraction is also tracked — every time a PM confirms an AI-filled field, the system saves that as a training example so the same vendor's future documents auto-fill more accurately.
